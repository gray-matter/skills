# Skills repo — agent guide

## Skill changes

- After changing a skill or its supporting files, ask an independent review subagent to assess whether the new or changed instructions are relevant, clear, and worth their context cost. Choose a capable model and reasoning effort appropriate to the change; use high reasoning for substantial changes.
- Have the reviewer suggest specific edits. The author makes or answers each suggestion, then asks the reviewer to reassess. Repeat until author and reviewer agree the change is focused and ready; summarize any remaining disagreement instead of silently treating it as approval.
- Keep the review focused on the diff and the skill's intended use. Do not expand scope into unrelated skills or documentation.
